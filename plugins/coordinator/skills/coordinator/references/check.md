# Check

Rules for the path "Check". The order of steps is in SKILL.md.

- The mode phrase always contains "I change nothing unless you ask".
- The researcher reads the thing and retells it without judgment. The person confirms the retelling. For someone else's thing, confirm by two or three examples of its output.
- The result tester walks it as a user against the retelling. Actions that write to other systems or send to people go to a test recipient only. If access is missing, ask the person.
- The report to the person is one of three: "ready to use", "usable with care, here is what", "not ready yet, here is why", plus what was checked, what was not and why, and how deep the check went.
- Fixes only on the person's request, and then it is improvement.
