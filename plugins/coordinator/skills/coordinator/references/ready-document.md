# Ready document or work plan

Rules for a person who comes with a ready brief, PRD, spec or work plan. The order of steps is in SKILL.md.

- A ready brief or PRD: the researcher copies it and checks its claims about the environment and data by reading. The document checker gets the document alone and looks for places that contradict each other.
- Retell the document's key decisions in one message, in the person's words: input, output, how often, what on failure, who gets what, what costs money. Mark separately what looks like someone else's choice rather than the person's goal: "the document has this, do you want it?".
- A good document gets one "yes". Questions only where a simpler or cheaper path was found, where the document contradicts itself, or where the PRD lacks something: one at a time, with a recommendation. Confirmed things are not re-asked.
- A ready spec is material: extract what it says about behaviour, show it in one pass, do not re-ask what is filled, confirm the brief and PRD, and let the composer write the spec anew from the PRD, carrying over build decisions as material if they still matter.
- A spec together with a brief or PRD: the documents go by the points above, the spec by the previous point.
- A work plan or a description of a situation is input material. Help the person see which product solves the question, then build it by the main path.
