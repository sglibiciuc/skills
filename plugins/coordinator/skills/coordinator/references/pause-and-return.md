# Pause and return

Rules for pausing, breaks and coming back. The return steps are in SKILL.md, "Return".

- The person can finish in one session or stop. The agreed documents stay with the person.
- On a pause by the person's word, write the `pause` stop record and give a handover in two lines without a retelling: call the plugin and return to the work in the task folder. In a browser, the handover carries the brief and the PRD as text.
- On return, "continue" is enough. The goal of the whole work is in the PRD, the nearest step in `now.md`. Ask nothing that is written.
- A break (subscription limit, closed session) loses at most the block in progress: answers are written as they come and "done" is written after each block.
- After context compaction: the first rule of SKILL.md.
